# 🧭 Your FDE positioning

> [!TIP]
> **🎯 One-line version**
>
> I am a software engineer who has moved from dependable backend systems into applied AI, and I enjoy staying responsible for the whole path: understanding the user's problem, building the smallest useful solution, integrating it into real workflows, and improving it from evidence.

---

## 💪 Why this is credible for you

| | Strength | Evidence |
|:---:|---|---|
| 🏗️ | **Systems foundation** | Banking and airline systems taught you to respect existing services, data flows, operational constraints and failure recovery |
| 🚀 | **Applied AI ownership** | Poultry 360, Aaina and Aura with Rav turn a real need into a deployed product, not just a notebook or demo |
| 🛡️ | **Reliability mindset** | Evaluations catching routing errors, preserving user dislikes, guarding against invented numbers, security checks, human approval |
| 🌏 | **Customer context** | Poultry 360's low-literacy, multilingual design: the interface and operating context matter as much as the model |
| 🔀 | **Learning in public** | 112 merged PRs: working in unfamiliar codebases, responding to review |

## 📖 The transition story

> [!TIP]
> **🗣️ Say this (≈45 s)**
>
> "I started with backend and distributed systems, where correctness and integration matter because other systems and people depend on them. As I moved into AI, I kept the same production mindset. I built systems around actual workflows, added evaluations and guardrails when I found failure cases, and shipped the products for real users. That combination of customer problem-solving and engineering through deployment is why I'm targeting Forward Deployed Engineering."

## 🚧 Honest boundaries

> [!WARNING]
> - Say **"I built"** only for work you personally did; name collaborators and shared ownership.
> - For enterprise work, explain your own responsibility **without** confidential customer or company details.
> - For each metric, be ready to say **what was measured, over what period and how it changed**.
> - Your CV says **4+ years** and lists roles from 2022. Use one consistent calculation, and don't switch between "4+" and "5 years" without explaining the dates.
