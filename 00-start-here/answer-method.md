# 🧱 The CLEAR answer method

Use **CLEAR** for any project, technical or customer question:

| Letter | Step | Ask yourself |
|:---:|---|---|
| 🅲 | **Context** | Who had the problem? What was happening? |
| 🅻 | **Limitation** | What made it hard? Which constraints mattered? |
| 🅴 | **Engineering choice** | What did you choose, and why? What did you not choose yet? |
| 🅰 | **Assurance** | How did you test, measure, secure, deploy or monitor it? |
| 🆁 | **Result & reflection** | What changed? What would you improve next? |

> [!TIP]
> Keep the first answer to **about one minute**, then let the interviewer probe. A good answer is not a speech. It's a clear starting point for a technical conversation.

---

## 🤔 When you don't know

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **🗣️ Fill in the blanks. This is stronger than bluffing.**
>
> "I haven't implemented that exact approach. My current understanding is ___. I would verify ___, then compare it against ___ using ___. In my own system, the closest related decision was ___."

---

## 🧪 For system design or customer cases

Start with the **user and the decision**, not a tool name:

- [ ] 👤 Who is the user, and what are they trying to do?
- [ ] 📍 What happens today, and where does it fail or cost time?
- [ ] 🚧 What systems, data, permissions, connectivity, language or budget constraints exist?
- [ ] ✂️ What is the smallest useful end-to-end slice?
- [ ] 💥 What can go wrong, and how will the user recover?
- [ ] 📊 What evidence would show it is useful and safe?
- [ ] 🚀 How will we roll it out, train users and support it?

> [!NOTE]
> Only then choose APIs, models, agents, retrieval or infrastructure.
