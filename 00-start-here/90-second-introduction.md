# 🎤 "Tell me about yourself" — 90 seconds

> [!TIP]
> **🗣️ Ready answer**
>
> "Hi, I'm Rav Chandan Kumar Singh. I'm a software engineer focused on building AI products that work in real workflows. I began with backend and distributed systems in banking and aviation, including airline services handling over 500,000 transactions a day. That gave me a strong base in APIs, events, reliability and integration.
>
> Over the last few years I've applied that foundation to AI. I built and run products such as Poultry 360, which helps poultry farmers count birds and get advice in multiple Indian languages; Aaina, an AI consultation product for salons; and Aura with Rav, which uses safety checks and human approval. I use evaluations to catch failures rather than trusting a good-looking demo. For example, testing exposed language-routing and preference-preservation failures, and I turned those into explicit checks.
>
> I've also had 140 pull requests merged into open-source projects, which taught me to work in unfamiliar code and take maintainer feedback well. I'm targeting FDE roles because I like being close to the user's problem, building with them, integrating into their environment, and staying accountable until the solution is useful and reliable."

## 🧩 Remember the sequence, not the sentences

| 1️⃣ | ➡️ | 2️⃣ | ➡️ | 3️⃣ | ➡️ | 4️⃣ |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 🏗️ Systems foundation | | 🤖 Applied AI | | 📊 Production evidence | | 🎯 Why FDE |

Keep it near **75–90 seconds** and leave space for the interviewer to pick a thread.

## 🪜 Follow-up ladder

**❓ Follow-ups** · *click to open*

<details>
<summary><b>Which product best represents you?</b></summary>

| If the interviewer values… | Use |
|---|---|
| User discovery, multilingual access, practical constraints, computer vision | 🐔 Poultry 360 |
| Agent orchestration, safety, human review | 🔮 Aura with Rav |
| Workflow design and evaluation | 💇 Aaina |

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What did you personally own?</b></summary>

State exactly what you designed, coded, deployed, evaluated and operated. Separate your work from any borrowed service, model, library or other person's contribution.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What was the hardest part?</b></summary>

Name a **real failure mode**, how you found it, what you changed and how you checked the fix. Don't claim the model was simply "more accurate" without a measured result.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why FDE rather than a standard AI engineer role?</b></summary>

> "I want a role where engineering includes customer context and integration, not only model or API implementation. I already enjoy taking a problem from an unclear request to a working solution, and I want to do that directly with customers and their constraints."

</details>
