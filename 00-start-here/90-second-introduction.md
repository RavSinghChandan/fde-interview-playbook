# “Tell me about yourself” — 90 seconds

## Ready answer

“Hi, I’m Rav Chandan Kumar Singh. I’m a software engineer focused on building AI products that work in real workflows. I began with backend and distributed systems in banking and aviation, including airline services handling over 500,000 transactions a day. That gave me a strong base in APIs, events, reliability, and integration.

Over the last few years I’ve applied that foundation to AI. I built and run products such as Poultry 360, which helps poultry farmers count birds and get advice in multiple Indian languages; Aaina, an AI consultation product for salons; and Aura with Rav, which uses safety checks and human approval. I use evaluations to catch failures rather than trusting a good-looking demo. For example, testing exposed language-routing and preference-preservation failures, and I turned those into explicit checks.

I’ve also had 106 pull requests merged into open-source projects, which taught me to work in unfamiliar code and take maintainer feedback well. I’m targeting FDE roles because I like being close to the user's problem, building with them, integrating into their environment, and staying accountable until the solution is useful and reliable.”

## Follow-up ladder

**“Which product best represents you?”**

Use Poultry 360 if the interviewer values user discovery, multilingual access, practical constraints, or applied computer vision. Use Aura with Rav if the interviewer values agent orchestration, safety, or human review. Use Aaina for workflow design and evaluation.

**“What did you personally own?”**

State the exact parts you designed, coded, deployed, evaluated, and operated. Separate your work from any borrowed service, model, library, or contribution by another person.

**“What was the hardest part?”**

Name a real failure mode, how you found it, what you changed, and how you checked the fix. Avoid claiming the model was simply “more accurate” unless you have a measured result.

**“Why FDE rather than a standard AI engineer role?”**

“I want a role where engineering includes customer context and integration, not only model or API implementation. I already enjoy taking a problem from an unclear request through a working solution, and I want to do that directly with customers and their constraints.”

## Practice rule

Remember the sequence, not every sentence: **systems foundation → applied AI → production evidence → why FDE**. Keep it near 75–90 seconds and leave space for the interviewer to choose a thread.
