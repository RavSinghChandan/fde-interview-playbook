# FDE Interview Playbook — Rav Chandan Kumar Singh

A practical, personal preparation system for Forward Deployed Engineering interviews. Start with a 10-minute answer, then follow the likely probes until you can explain the decision, evidence, and trade-off in your own words.

> This is a living interview notebook, not a transcript dump or a claim that any company will ask these exact questions. Bootcamp notes are translated into practice material; answers are grounded in the candidate's CV and public work. Replace any detail that is outdated or confidential before sharing.

## Start in 10 minutes

1. Read [your positioning](00-start-here/positioning.md).
2. Practice the [90-second introduction](00-start-here/90-second-introduction.md) aloud once, without memorizing it word for word.
3. Pick one story from the [story bank](03-story-bank/README.md) and answer: problem, your action, evidence, trade-off, learning.
4. Use [the answer method](00-start-here/answer-method.md) when a follow-up surprises you.

## Study paths

| Time | What to do |
|---|---|
| 10 minutes | One short answer + three follow-up questions |
| 30 minutes | One interview story, then a skeptical follow-up round |
| 60 minutes | One case: clarify the customer problem, design a thin solution, test failure modes, explain rollout |
